FAQ
How do I cancel a booking? +
My Tickets → select booking → Cancel. Refunds in 5–7 business days.
My QR code isn't scanning +
Increase screen brightness, hold steady. Show booking reference to event staff.
Can I transfer my ticket? +
Transfer eligibility depends on organiser policy. Check the event page or contact support.
When will I get my refund? +
5–7 business days to original payment. Wallet refunds are instant.
How do I become a creator? +
Profile → Switch to Creator Mode → complete KYC. Approval takes 24–48 hours.
